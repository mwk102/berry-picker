# Northwest U-Pick

## Vision

Northwest U-Pick helps people answer one simple question:

> **Where should I go picking today?**

Planning a U-pick trip should be exciting—not frustrating.

Today, people often have to search dozens of farm websites, Facebook pages, Instagram posts, and outdated directories just to answer a few simple questions.

We believe planning a farm visit should take minutes, not hours.

Northwest U-Pick exists to bring everything together into one trusted place.

---

# The Problem

When planning a U-pick trip, people almost always ask the same four questions.

## 📍 Where are the farms?

People shouldn't have to search Google Maps, Facebook, and farm websites separately.

Northwest U-Pick provides one searchable map of seasonal farms and agricultural experiences.

---

## 🍓 What can I pick right now?

Availability changes constantly.

Blueberries may be ending.

Raspberries may be just beginning.

Pumpkins may not open for another month.

We help people discover what's actually available today.

---

## 🕒 Are they open?

Hours change.

Weather changes.

Seasonal closures happen.

Many farms close early when fields are picked over.

Visitors should know before leaving home.

---

## 💲 How much does it cost?

Pricing is surprisingly difficult to find.

Visitors shouldn't have to browse multiple websites just to compare farms.

Northwest U-Pick makes pricing easy to discover.

# Product Philosophy

Northwest U-Pick is not a business directory.

It is a decision-making tool.

Every screen should help visitors confidently choose where to spend their day.

---

# Core Principles

## Accuracy

Information should come from trusted sources whenever possible.

Every update should include:

- who submitted it
- when it was updated
- whether it has been verified

Trust is a feature.

---

## Freshness

Static information is useful.

Current information is invaluable.

Whenever possible we display:

- current crop availability
- today's picking conditions
- today's prices
- today's announcements
- today's weather
- today's photos

---

## Simplicity

Visitors should never have to dig for basic information.

The most important answers should be visible immediately.

---

## Family First

Families often care about different things than experienced pickers.

We highlight information like:

- restrooms
- parking
- accessibility
- stroller friendliness
- food
- farm stores
- play areas
- wagon rides

Planning a family outing should be effortless.

---

## Community

Communities keep information fresh.

Visitors help answer questions that websites often cannot.

Examples include:

- How crowded is it?
- Are blueberries picked over?
- Is the parking lot full?
- Are fields muddy?

Community reports complement—not replace—verified farm information.
