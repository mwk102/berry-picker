# Bell's Farm Reference Profile

## Why This Farm Is a Reference Farm

Bell's Farm is a Whidbey Island farm near Coupeville with an official website, farm stand, flowers, strawberries, vegetables, and regenerative farming practices. It is a useful reference case because it is source-backed as a real farm, but its U-pick berry status is not yet confirmed from the available official pages.

## Verified Facts

- Official name: Bell's Farm
- City/state: Coupeville, WA
- Address: 892 West Beach Rd, Coupeville, WA 98239
- Phone: 360-678-4808
- Website: https://www.bells-farm.com/
- Official site describes Bell's as a family farm since 1946.
- Official site confirms farm stand products and says to watch for U-pick flower season.
- Official site says the farm is not certified organic and does not use synthetic chemicals, fertilizers, pesticides, or herbicides on produce.
- Whidbey Island Grown profile confirms the farm is on West Beach Rd near Coupeville.
- Whidbey Island Grown profile confirms strawberries, vegetables, fruiting trees, and pasture-raised livestock.
- Whidbey Island Grown profile says products can be purchased at the farm stand at 892 West Beach Road and through the food hub.
- Sources were re-reviewed July 7, 2026. Current U-pick berry availability remains unconfirmed.
