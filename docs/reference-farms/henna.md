# Henna Blueberry Farm Reference Profile

## Why This Farm Is a Reference Farm

Henna Blueberry Farm is a focused blueberry U-pick farm in the Snoqualmie River Valley. It is a good reference farm for single-crop seasonal availability, crop variety notes, and explicit uncertainty around missing prices and hours.

## Verified Facts

- Official name: Henna Blueberry Farm
- City/state: Carnation, WA
- Address: 32500 NE 60th Street, Carnation, WA 98014
- Website: https://hennablueberryfarm.com/
- Crop: blueberry
- Official site describes U-pick blueberries in the Snoqualmie River Valley.
- Official site lists Duke, Reka, and Draper among its primary blueberry varieties.
- Official site states the farm is not certified organic and describes low-spray/sustainable growing practices.
- Official U-pick page lists blueberry U-pick at $3.90/lb.
- Official U-pick page lists Monday closed.
- Official U-pick page lists Tuesday through Thursday morning and evening picking windows.
- Official U-pick page lists Friday through Sunday morning picking windows.
- Official U-pick page says fields may close early when picked out and recommends calling ahead on weekends.
